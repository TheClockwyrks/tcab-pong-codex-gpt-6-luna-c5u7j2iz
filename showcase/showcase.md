A Carom match opens with a brief pause before the first serve. Then two paddles face off across a dark field, trading a bright ball through a pair of fixed obstacles. Every clean return makes the ball faster, turning a calm opening rally into a test of quick reads.

Move into the ball with intent: your paddle's movement at impact adds spin, curving the flight after it leaves. A well timed strike can bend around an obstacle or turn a difficult return into a sharp bank shot.

Score by sending the ball beyond your opponent. The obstacles reward planning, while the top and bottom walls offer another route back across the field. Matches go to eleven, and close games continue until someone leads by two.

Choose Solo to face the computer or Versus to share the keyboard. Each point resets the ball for a new serve, so a match stays focused on the next angle, the next touch, and the next chance to take control.
